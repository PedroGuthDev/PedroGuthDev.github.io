# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential clients, recruiters, founders, and collaborators evaluating Pedro Guth as a full-stack product engineer. They arrive with limited attention and need quick proof that Pedro can design, build, ship, and communicate clearly across both front-end and back-end work.

## Product Purpose

This site is Pedro Guth's personal portfolio and contact surface. It should establish credibility, show selected shipped projects, clarify the stack, and make it easy to start a conversation about projects, roles, freelance work, or collaboration.

## Brand Personality

Precise, practical, and quietly confident. The voice should feel like a builder who cares about product craft, not a generic developer profile.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dark SaaS glassmorphism, template-like "developer portfolio" sections, endless identical cards, oversized abstract decoration with no purpose, and visual effects that compete with the actual project work.

## Design Principles

- Show shipped work before decoration.
- Make technical range legible without turning the page into a stack badge wall.
- Treat contact as a serious conversion moment, not an afterthought.
- Keep the interface bilingual and readable on mobile.
- Let craft appear through spacing, typography, image treatment, and interaction details.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, clear focus states, reduced-motion support, descriptive alt text, and resilient responsive layouts for small mobile screens through wide desktop monitors.
